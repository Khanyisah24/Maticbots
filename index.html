Where your subjects can take you

Four faculties, one campus

Every qualification at UNIZULU sits inside one of these four faculties. The Chatbot uses this exact structure to line up your subjects with real study options.

Three steps

How the Chatbot matches you

Tell it your subjects

Pick your Grade 12 subjects and an honest sense of your marks — by chip, by typing, or by speaking, in English or isiZulu.

It weighs the fit

The Chatbot checks your subjects and marks against the indicative APS and subject requirements for programmes in all 4 faculties.

You explore, in plain language

You get 2–3 ranked qualifications with why they fit, what they require, and what to ask next — no jargon, no dead ends.

Meet the chatbot

The Chatbot speaks your language — both of them

  • EN/ZU

    Bilingual by design

    Ask in English or isiZulu — the Chatbot recognises greetings, subjects and grades in both, and replies in the language you used.

  • 🎙

    Voice in, voice out

    Tap the microphone to speak your subjects instead of typing, and let the Chatbot read recommendations back to you.

  • ✓

    Grounded in real requirements

    Every suggestion is tied to an indicative APS band and subject minimum — flagged clearly as "confirm in the official prospectus" where numbers vary.

  • +

    Built to grow

    The language engine is structured so isiXhosa, Sesotho and Afrikaans can be added as extra recognised languages, not a rebuild.
